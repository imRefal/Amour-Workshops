Our Story ♡

Amour Workshops began with a simple dream shared by two girls who love creativity, art, and bringing people together.

We wanted to create more than just workshops. We dreamed of a special place where women could relax, discover new hobbies, and make beautiful memories.

That dream became Amour Workshops, a private creative experience for women hosted at Amour Café in Al-Baha.

What Makes Amour Special ♡

  • One of the first creative workshop experiences of its kind in Al-Baha.
  • Located inside Amour Café, where visitors can enjoy café services alongside creative activities.
  • Offers creative workshops and activities suitable for different age groups.
  • Available for private bookings, including birthdays, celebrations, and special occasions.
  • A welcoming space for creativity, relaxation, and beautiful memories.
